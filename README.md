# Sales & Profit Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end sales and profitability analysis using Microsoft Excel.

The objective was to clean and analyze sales data, calculate key performance indicators, identify business trends, and create a management dashboard to support data-driven decision-making.

The analysis focuses on revenue, product costs, profit, and profit margins across regions, categories, and products.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:

1. What causes revenue to differ between regions?
2. Which product generates the highest revenue?
3. Which product generates the lowest revenue?
4. Which region generates the lowest revenue?
5. Which products, categories, and regions require further investigation?
6. How can the business identify opportunities to improve profitability?

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains sales transaction information including:

- Order ID
- Date
- Customer
- Region
- Product
- Category
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Payment Method
- Total Sales
- Unit Cost
- Profit
- Profit Margin

The dataset is a sample dataset created for analytical practice and does not represent the financial performance of a real company.

---

## Data Cleaning

The data-cleaning process focused on:

- Identifying duplicate records
- Checking missing values
- Reviewing incorrect or inconsistent data
- Standardizing text and capitalization
- Reviewing unusual quantities
- Checking product-cost information
- Preparing the data for profitability analysis

## Key Findings

### Regional Performance

- Dubai generated the highest revenue at AED 11,400.
- Dubai also generated the highest total profit at AED 4,000.
- Abu Dhabi generated the lowest revenue at AED 2,200.

### Product Performance

- Laptop generated the highest revenue at AED 17,500.
- Mouse generated the lowest revenue at AED 400.
- Laptop generated the highest total profit at AED 3,500.
- Chair had the highest product profit margin at 44.44%.

### Category Performance

- Electronics generated the highest revenue at AED 23,150.
- Electronics also generated the highest total profit at AED 5,475.
- Furniture had a higher profit margin than Electronics: 41.44% compared with 23.65%.

---

## Recommendations

### 1. Investigate Laptop Profitability

Laptop sales generate significant revenue and profit, but the product has a 20% profit margin.

The business should investigate supplier and purchasing costs to determine whether lower product costs could improve the margin while maintaining sales volume.

### 2. Investigate Furniture Profitability

Furniture has a higher profit margin than Electronics.

Management should investigate the factors contributing to this stronger margin and assess whether similar pricing or cost strategies could be applied to other products.

### 3. Investigate Regional Performance

Dubai generated the highest revenue and profit.

Management could investigate the factors behind Dubai's stronger performance and assess whether successful approaches can be tested in other regions.

---

## Limitations

The analysis has several limitations:

- The dataset is a sample dataset and does not represent a real company's financial performance.
- Full operating expenses are not available, so true net profit cannot be calculated.
- Cash-flow data is not available.
- Customer retention and churn data are not available.
- Marketing expenditure and customer acquisition data are not available.
- Supplier information is not available.
- The dataset covers a limited period and cannot establish long-term growth trends.
